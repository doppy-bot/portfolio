Credit Risk Decision System

Gradient-boosted credit risk model with SHAP explainability, built so a loan officer can see exactly why a decision was made — not just what it was.

Payment Analytics A/B Pipeline

dbt + DuckDB pipeline over 120K synthetic payment transactions, testing a fraud-check flow against a control group — 24 passing dbt tests, 46% fraud reduction measured with SciPy/statsmodels.

dbtDuckDBSciPystatsmodels

LangChainLLaMA 3.2RAG
